Lightroom Classic plugin

Auto Photo Edit

A plugin that learned how I edit, crop and straighten swing-dance photos, from five years of my own edits.

Learning from my catalog

one-time setup, on my own PC
What it learns from
My Lightroom catalog

"Before" images
13,198 untouched previews from the DNG files, plus the camera's white balance
Photo features
Brightness, clipping, colour cast, camera settings and a scene description
People in the frame

What it learns
Tone model
Exposure & white balance
How each photo should differ from the event's key shots under changing dance-floor light
Subject classifier
Who is the couple?
Tells the dancers apart from blurry bystanders, learned from 67,213 people in my crops
Crop model
How I frame a couple
Headroom, feet, side space and portrait, square or wide, learned from 7,987 of my crops
Straightening
Which way is up
A pretrained model (GeoCalib) estimates camera tilt, tuned to how much I usually rotate

What happens to each photo

about a second per photo
A Copy my look
The key photos' average contrast, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, clarity, dehaze, sharpening, noise reduction and vignette
B Correct the light
The tone model adjusts exposure and white balance for each photo individually
C Find the couple
Pose detection plus the subject classifier pick the dancers I'm photographing
D Frame them
The crop model frames the couple my way, and a rule pushes out any edge that would cut off heads or feet
E Straighten
Rotates by the estimated camera tilt; tiny tilts under half a degree are left alone
F Apply in Lightroom
Only Lightroom's own settings change. Key photos and existing crops are never touched
